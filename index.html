06 — FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Do I need technical knowledge to work with you?

Not at all. You know your business; we handle everything technical. You'll get plain-English updates and a simple dashboard to see what your automations are doing.

How fast will I see results?

Most clients have their first automation live within 2–3 weeks. Many see it pay for itself in the first month.

Is my data safe?

Yes. We follow least-privilege access, encrypt data in transit and at rest, can deploy inside your own cloud, and will sign an NDA/DPA. We never use your data to train public models.

Will AI replace my team?

Our goal is to remove the repetitive work so your people can focus on the parts that need a human — relationships, judgement and creativity.

What if something breaks?

Every build includes monitoring and alerting. On support or retainer plans we fix issues before you notice — usually within hours.
