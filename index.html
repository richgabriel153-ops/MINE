FAQ

Questions,
answered.

How do we get started?

Book a free call or send a message using the form below. After a short conversation about your goals, we send a written proposal with the scope, timeline and a fixed price. Work begins once you approve it.

Do I need any technical knowledge?

No. You explain how your business works and we handle the technical side. You receive plain-English updates, a walkthrough video and documentation your team can follow.

Which tools do you work with?

OpenAI and Anthropic models, Make, Zapier, n8n, HubSpot, Google Workspace, Airtable, Notion, Shopify, Slack, WhatsApp Business, Twilio and Supabase, plus custom APIs. If a tool has an API, we can usually connect it.

How long does a project take?

Starter projects take 3 to 5 days, Business 7 to 10 days and Scale 14 to 21 days. Your proposal confirms the exact timeline before we start.

How does payment work?

For direct projects we take 50% to start and 50% on delivery, by bank transfer, card or PayPal. Orders placed through Fiverr are paid securely on Fiverr.

Who owns what you build?

You do. We build inside your own accounts wherever possible and hand over all code, workflows, prompts and documentation when the project is paid in full.

Is my data safe?

We use the minimum access needed, keep credentials in secure storage and never use your data to train public AI models. We are happy to sign an NDA before you share sensitive information.

Are there ongoing costs?

Some tools charge their own usage or subscription fees, and we tell you exactly what to expect before the build. Our care plan is optional and can be cancelled at any time.

What happens if something stops working?

Fixes are free during your support period. After that, the care plan covers monitoring and repairs, or we can quote individual fixes as needed.
