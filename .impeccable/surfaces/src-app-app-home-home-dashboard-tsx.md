# App shell and Home (InCeipt)

Scope: the signed-in app (Home, History, New, Customers, More, receipt view) and the app icon. Mode: Operate.
Audience and job: market and shop owners on cheap Android phones, often outdoors; make a receipt and send it on WhatsApp in seconds, and see who owes them.
Constraints: Lagoon palette and Atkinson Hyperlegible are brand commitments; documents carry the seller's brand, not ours; 48px targets; AA contrast minimum.

## Direction contract

FORM: Nigerian hand-painted shop signboard, fused into a standard mobile app shell. seed:44a65695a2fc3661
THESIS: The seller's shop sign in their pocket: Home opens on their own painted signboard (their name, logo, colour) with the money and the next action under it. Refuses the grey fintech dashboard of balance card + icon grid.
OWN-WORLD: Deep teal fields that own the top of each screen; coral reserved for the one action that makes money (New receipt / Send); near-black ink outlines (2px) on primary panels and buttons like painted sign borders; white panels on a pale lagoon ground; heavy Atkinson at 800 for names and amounts, tight caps for stamps; PAID / PART PAID / OWING as bold rectangular stamps in solid state colours.
STORY: Who I am (signboard), what came in and who owes me, then one big coral "New receipt" sign, then recent sales.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Teal signboard panel with logo and business name set large; under it, this week's takings and the owed total in the same panel; a full-width coral New receipt button with ink outline; recent rows below.
SIGNATURE: The signboard: a painted-sign panel (teal field, ink frame, inner keyline) carrying the seller's identity; it reappears as the header of every main screen and as the app icon's frame.
RISK: Louder than typical apps; ink outlines must stay calm on long lists, so lists use hairlines and only primary panels get the 2px ink frame.
