Case study

InCeipt: receipts, invoices and an AI assistant for small businesses

A mobile-first app that lets business owners send professional receipts on WhatsApp in seconds, then grows with them into payments, staff accounts and AI-assisted bookkeeping.

Market
Nigerian SMEs
Platform
Installable web app
Pro plan
₦3,000 / month
Stack
Next.js, Supabase

The challenge

Many small businesses in Nigeria still write receipts by hand or send plain text messages to confirm payments. That makes them look less professional, makes records hard to keep, and leaves owners without a clear view of sales, debts and profit.

The goal was a tool that a shop owner could use on a basic phone in under a minute, without creating an account, and that could grow into a paid product for businesses that need more.

What we built

A free tool that works in seconds

  • Receipts and invoices with the business name, logo and bank details on every document
  • Automatic totals, discounts, delivery fees, VAT and part payments, calculated in kobo to avoid rounding errors
  • One-tap sharing to WhatsApp as a light image, plus PDF download
  • No sign-up: records are stored on the phone and the app works offline once installed

A Pro plan that pays for itself

  • Monthly and yearly subscriptions through Paystack, with webhooks keeping accounts in sync
  • Pay Now links on invoices, so customers pay by card or transfer directly into the business account
  • Staff accounts with owner controls and an activity log
  • Expense tracking, a profit view, quotations and proforma invoices
  • An admin dashboard showing revenue, growth, usage and payment events

An AI assistant that takes action safely

The InCeipt Assistant lets an owner type requests such as "Record ₦32,500 from Ada's Kitchen, paid by transfer". Built on the Claude API with tool use, it prepares the record and asks for confirmation before saving anything. It also answers tax questions based on the Nigeria Tax Act 2025. A daily usage limit keeps AI costs predictable.

How it is built

  • Front end: Next.js App Router with Tailwind CSS, installable as a progressive web app
  • Data: IndexedDB on the device for free users, Supabase with row-level security for Pro accounts
  • Payments: Paystack subscriptions, subaccounts and verified webhooks
  • AI: Claude API with tool use and confirm-before-save
  • Quality: unit tests for money, totals, VAT and part payments, plus database tests
  • Compliance: Terms of Service, Privacy Policy aligned with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, and a Refund Policy
