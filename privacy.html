Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Aether AI Automation ("Aether", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects personal information when you visit our website, contact us or use our services. We process personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and, for clients in the European Union and United Kingdom, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email address, company name, budget and project details when you submit our contact form, email us, message us on WhatsApp or order through Fiverr.
  • Project information: access credentials, documents and business data you share so we can build your automation.
  • Technical information: our hosting provider may record standard server logs such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, for security and reliability.

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

2. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a proposal
  • To deliver, support and maintain the services you order
  • To send invoices and keep financial records required by law
  • To keep our website and systems secure

We do not sell your personal information, and we never use client data to train public AI models.

3. Legal basis

We process your information to take steps at your request before entering into a contract, to perform our contract with you, to meet legal obligations, and for our legitimate interest in running a secure business. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

4. Sharing your information

We share information only with service providers that help us run our business, such as website hosting, email, payment processors and the AI and automation platforms used in your project. These providers may process data outside your country. When they do, we use providers that offer appropriate safeguards.

5. How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become projects are deleted within 12 months. Project records and invoices are kept for as long as required by tax and accounting law. Access credentials are removed or returned to you when a project or support period ends.

6. Security

We use least-privilege access, store credentials in secure password managers, enable two-factor authentication where available and sign non-disclosure agreements on request.

7. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, or object to or restrict how we use it. To make a request, email hello@yourdomain.com. We respond within 30 days. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission or your local data protection authority.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
